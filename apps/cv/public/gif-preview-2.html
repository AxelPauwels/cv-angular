GIF preview 2: seamless loops and joins (temporary)

Every GIF is drawn frame by frame (ImageDecoder) so only the part that loops plays. The ranges were found by comparing every frame with every other one (mean pixel difference at 48×48; for scale: one walking frame to the next differs about 1.8, three frames 5.4).

Joined sequences

The GIFs don't share one camera: the figure is 228 to 288 px tall and stands at a different place in each one. So every GIF is scaled and moved once, so that its first frame lines up (feet, centre, height) with the frame it follows; after that it keeps that placement, so a sequence can loop. Each cut is at the pair of frames that look most alike.

Seamless loops (single GIF)

"Whole GIF": its last frame flows back into the first. "Frames a–b": the GIF has a lead-in or lead-out, so only that stretch repeats (frame b+1 ≈ frame a). Difference = last frame back to the first of the loop.
